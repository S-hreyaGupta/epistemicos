<objective>
Collapse the `ci.yml` `go` job onto `make gate`, bind the compose PostgreSQL to
loopback, guard the one unguarded heading index in the repository, and document the
gate's new shape and the escalation flag where a developer will meet them.

`internal/platform/`. Each is small on its own; together they are what makes the
strict gate 02-01 built into a single definition that CI shares, on a service that
is not reachable from the local network, with documentation that is true.

a length guard, and a corrected README.
