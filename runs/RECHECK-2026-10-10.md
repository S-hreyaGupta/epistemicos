## The four-cycle limit

On 9 October a killed probe run left a mutation in `scripts/loop_state.py`
that replaced `if n >= MAX_VALID_CYCLES:` with `if False:`, disabling the
ceiling. It was found in the working tree the same day and restored; HEAD was
never affected, which the diff at the time established.

Two things rule out any effect on the recorded results, and they are
independent of each other.

First, direction. A disabled ceiling cannot produce `MAX_4_REACHED`. With that
branch switched off the controller falls through to `CONTINUE`. Both of the
runs that report the ceiling therefore reported it with the ceiling working;
the answer could not have been produced otherwise.

Second, reproduction. The three results above were computed today on clean
code and are identical to the ones on record. BOOTSTRAP-003's `STALLED` never
reaches the ceiling test at all, since §6 evaluates the stall before the
budget, so it is unaffected either way.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

It establishes that the three recorded outcomes are what the current, verified
code produces from the current evidence. It does not establish that the
evidence itself is sound; that is what MC-2 and the ledger checks are for, and
they run inside the controller before it will state any outcome at all.

It also does not establish that nothing else was affected by that mutation
during the window it was applied. What can be said precisely: the window was
roughly 17:20 to 17:30 on 9 October, nothing was committed in it, and the only
tool that reads `MAX_VALID_CYCLES` is the controller, whose three outputs are
reproduced above.
