## 42.1 Purpose and ownership

Step 9 answers exactly one question: **"Do two or more independently extracted gap instances represent the same canonical manuscript-asserted gap?"** Its output determines whether (A) multiple assertions collapse into one canonical `gap_record`; (B) two canonical records remain separate with `possibly_same_claim` links; (C) two canonical records remain separate with `related_claim` links; (D) two records remain separate with no link; (E) a persistence-reason deficiency resolves to an existing canonical gap.

Step 9 OWNS: gap identity; affected-object equivalence FOR IDENTITY PURPOSES; complete-proposition equivalence; restatement attachment; merge decisions; canonical-representative selection; identity clustering; `possibly_same_claim`; `related_claim`; the `unmerged_basis_code` vocabulary; the `relation_basis_code` vocabulary; persistence-reason identity reconciliation. Step 9 does NOT own extraction of: GAP boundary (Step 5); relation and knowledge_kind (Step 6); affected_object (Step 8); scope/context (external capability); candidate detection (Step 10); scientific truth; novelty; citation validity; evidence support.

## 42.2 Frozen inputs

Every identity comparison consumes only frozen upstream outputs: the Step-5 boundary class, Step-6 `(relation, knowledge_kind)`, the Step-8 `affected_object` (canonical fields plus Step-8 internal relationship/completion metadata where available), the complete preserved proposition (schema `proposition` block), spans, `gap_evidence`, `context_support`, `persistence_reasons`, `spawned_claim_ref`, and `claim_link` slots. Schema facts binding Step 9: `claim_link.link_type ∈ {possibly_same_claim, related_claim, reason_of}` (D4); `target_gap_id` must reference an existing record and can never equal the record's own `gap_id` (I-31); `unmerged_basis_code` is required iff `possibly_same_claim` and `relation_basis_code` iff `related_claim`, both drawn from the Step-9-owned `code(MERGE_RULES_SPEC)` slots (I-32); `source_persistence_reason_id` is required iff `reason_of` (I-29); symmetric-link reciprocity follows I-29; `gap_evidence.role ∈ {primary, restatement, corroborating}` with exactly one primary (I-1); `spawned_claim_ref` ABSENT means the reason asserts no independent deficiency (I-16). Step 9 never re-extracts, never repairs upstream classifications, and adds no canonical schema field.

## 42.3 Identity principle (frozen)

**GI-CORE-1 (SAME_GAP definition).** SAME_GAP(A,B) iff A.relation = B.relation AND A.knowledge_kind = B.knowledge_kind AND OBJECT_EQUIVALENT(A.affected_object, B.affected_object) AND PROPOSITION_EQUIVALENT(COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(A), COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(B)). All four conjuncts are mandatory.

**GI-CORE-2 (triple insufficiency).** The bare triple (relation, knowledge_kind, affected_object) is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT. Frozen counterexample: "Evidence about employee engagement is limited among frontline workers." vs "…among senior managers." share LIMITED · evidence · object "employee engagement" yet assert different source-grounded propositions → never merged. Contextual wording may be identity-relevant without becoming a structured A.1-E scope field: Step 9 compares the COMPLETE preserved proposition and owns no context extraction. No identity field named population, context, setting, geography, industry, time period, or level_of_analysis exists anywhere in Step 9.

**GI-CORE-3 (conservative default).** WHEN IN DOUBT, DO NOT MERGE. Prefer two separately reviewable canonical gaps over one falsely collapsed record. After records remain separate, routing follows the two-stage model: (A) IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED + registered POSSIBLE-A basis → `possibly_same_claim`; (B) DISTINCT_GAP + registered POSSIBLE-B plausible-core basis → `possibly_same_claim`; (C) DISTINCT_GAP + registered deterministic relatedness basis → `related_claim`; (D) otherwise → no link. No possibly_same or related link is ever emitted without its registered basis. This rule is fully aligned with GI-CORE-4, GI-CORE-7, GI-POSSIBLE-1, GI-RELATED-1, GI-LINK-2, and GI-PREC-1.

## 42.4 Identity decision model

**GI-CORE-4 (two-stage model).** `identity_decision ∈ {SAME_GAP, DISTINCT_GAP, IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED}` — Step-9 INTERNAL metadata, never silently added to `gap_record` (no such canonical field exists). The identity decision controls MERGE vs KEEP-SEPARATE **only**; claim-link routing is a separate, subsequent stage (Phase II, §42.42). Mapping: SAME_GAP → one canonical gap_record (merge), no link. IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED → keep separate → `possibly_same_claim` ONLY under a registered POSSIBLE-A basis (§42.27). DISTINCT_GAP → keep separate → then independently evaluate: (a) `possibly_same_claim` under a registered POSSIBLE-B "strict identity blocked but plausible shared/core gap" basis; (b) `related_claim` under a registered §42.45 basis; (c) otherwise no link. DISTINCT_GAP and IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED are never collapsed: DISTINCT_GAP = strict canonical identity is blocked; IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED = strict canonical identity cannot yet be determined. No `same_claim` link exists in the frozen schema: confirmed identity is represented ONLY as one canonical record with multiple evidence spans.

## 42.5 Comparison universe

**GI-CORE-5.** Identity comparison occurs only within the same `document_id` + `document_version` + run identity universe (the Step-4 candidate universe of the current closure epoch). Cross-document identity, cross-version manuscript persistence, and cross-run historical identity are OUT OF SCOPE — owned by the future Lifecycle Dependency, Versioning & Invalidation architecture, not built here.

**GI-CORE-6 (attribution and proposition-interface rule).** Step 5 already decided whether an attributed proposition functions as a manuscript GAP; Step 9 never reopens it. Step 9 compares COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(record) (§42.17), consuming the frozen upstream proposition representation exactly as supplied: `assertion_span_refs` anchor the local asserting wording; normalized/completed content and permitted `context_support` supply only source-grounded linguistic completion already authorized upstream. Step 9 never strips or reconstructs an attribution frame. CASE A — attribution lies outside `assertion_span_refs` AND is not part of the frozen completed representation: the frame is not part of the compared proposition, and Step 9 does not add it. CASE B — the attribution frame is inside `assertion_span_refs` (or otherwise retained in the frozen completed representation): it is material proposition content and CANNOT be erased by normalization (anti-erasure rule, §42.17) — typically → not SAME_GAP (at most `possibly_same_claim` · `proposition_qualification_difference` where its registered trigger is met). Source sentence equality is not the comparison unit; the complete frozen proposition representation is the comparison unit — Step 9 consumes that boundary and never creates it. Step-8 affected-object completion does not determine proposition-level attribution or proposition identity. Manuscript adoption of an attributed claim is never inferred by Step 9.

## 42.6 Relation equality

**GI-REL-1.** `relation` equality is EXACT over the frozen enum {ABSENT, LIMITED, CONFLICTING, UNTESTED, CONSTRAINED, DISCONNECTED, CHALLENGED}. Different values → NEVER SAME_GAP, with no weakening (ABSENT ↛ LIMITED) and no strengthening (LIMITED ↛ ABSENT) — Step-6 territory, frozen. "Evidence about X is limited." (LIMITED) vs "No evidence about X exists." (ABSENT) → DISTINCT even with identical object; eligible for `related_claim` · `same_object_different_relation` where §42.29 licenses it.

## 42.7 Knowledge-kind equality

**GI-KIND-1.** `knowledge_kind` equality is EXACT over {evidence, theory, method, practice_alignment, unspecified}. Different values → NEVER SAME_GAP: "Theoretical understanding of X is limited." (LIMITED · theory) vs "Evidence about X is limited." (LIMITED · evidence) → DISTINCT. **GI-KIND-2.** `unspecified` is a real frozen value, never a wildcard: LIMITED · unspecified does NOT merge with LIMITED · evidence; at most `related_claim` · `same_object_different_knowledge_kind` where licensed, or `possibly_same_claim` is NOT available on kind mismatch (identity is definitively blocked, not uncertain).

## 42.8 Object equivalence

**GI-OBJ-1.** OBJECT_EQUIVALENT(A,B) is decided ONLY from the frozen Step-8 output — never by re-extraction — and must be source-grounded, conservative, and deterministic under this versioned rule set. Confirmation requires at least one STRONG evidence class (§42.11) and no blocking conflict (§42.9, §42.10, §42.15, §42.16). Absence of strong evidence → equivalence unproven → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED at most (never confirmed by similarity). Confirmed non-equivalence (distinct source-grounded referents) → DISTINCT_GAP.

## 42.9 Object-kind compatibility

**GI-OBJ-2.** `object_kind` must match exactly for confirmed equivalence. On mismatch, Step 9 never silently chooses one classification and never repairs Step-8 output. Behavior: default DISTINCT-side handling with NO MERGE. `possibly_same_claim` · `object_kind_conflict` may be produced ONLY on SOURCE-GROUNDED COLLISION EVIDENCE stronger than lexical/topical resemblance — one of: (A) exact canonical mention surface after frozen safe normalization; (B) same proper/named designation; (C) an explicit manuscript alias/equivalence that nevertheless conflicts with upstream typing; (D) the same unique antecedent/coreference chain. NOT sufficient: same topical word; shared stem ("model" vs "modeling"); structurally similar sentences; general domain similarity. With collision evidence → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED → `possibly_same_claim` · `object_kind_conflict` + upstream review; without it → DISTINCT_GAP, no link. The mismatch itself is upstream's to fix (protocol defect classes), never Step 9's.

## 42.10 Artifact-kind compatibility

**GI-ART-1.** For `object_kind = knowledge_artifact`, `artifact_kind` must normally match over {proposition, theory, model, framework, assumption, measure, method}. theory vs model → not automatically equivalent; measure vs method → NEVER equivalent (frozen GS-1.3 §4a distinction); proposition vs relationship is already an object_kind difference. On artifact_kind mismatch: no merge; explicit manuscript aliasing (§42.13) is the ONLY route by which two differently labeled artifacts may contribute identity evidence, and even then aliasing that would force incompatible kinds (e.g., a measure aliased to a method) is rejected — Step 9 never scientifically relabels artifacts. Route as in GI-OBJ-2 — the same collision-evidence rule (A–D) applies verbatim — with `artifact_kind_conflict`; generic parallel sentences over kind-differing artifacts ("existing measures" vs "existing methods") carry NO collision evidence and yield DISTINCT_GAP with no link, preserving measure ≠ method without implying generic near-identity.

## 42.11 Strong object-equivalence evidence

**GI-OBJ-3 (closed strong-evidence classes).**
- **OE-1** — exact LOCALLY IDENTIFYING source-mention equality after only frozen safe normalization (OR-TRACE-5 whitelist: punctuation trim, whitespace, licensed case, licensed determiner removal; never broadened). Word-order or content differences are never normalized away. Preconditions: same object_kind; the mention is independently referential, OR any completion dependency has been separately proven compatible (GI-OBJ-8); no conflicting antecedent/coreference trace. Non-trigger: identical generic/anaphoric surface with different antecedents; identical generic/anaphoric surface with an unresolved antecedent; exact lexical identity across known distinct discourse referents. SAME SURFACE FORM ≠ SAME REFERENT.
- **OE-2** — one Step-8 completion/coreference chain explicitly resolves both mentions to the same source-grounded antecedent (same antecedent span per I-44 completion traces).
- **OE-3** — the manuscript itself explicitly states an alias/equivalence ("Model A, hereafter the adaptation model") satisfying §42.13.
- **OE-4** — the same proper/named artifact designation appears in both assertions ("Model M", "Theory X", "Assumption 1").
- **OE-5** — relationship objects have source-grounded structurally equivalent arguments/direction/qualifier under §42.15, including only the closed predicate-normalization registry.
- **OE-6** — registered lossless grammatical transformation of the affected-object surface form (**GI-OBJ-7**). Applies ONLY when: (1) both Step-8 objects share the same object_kind; (2) the transformation is listed in the CLOSED object-transformation registry (§42.22 sub-registries: currently OE-T-NOMV only); (3) all semantic participants and modifiers are preserved; (4) no material contextual restriction changes; (5) no external synonym inference occurs. OE-6 is not a general nominalization engine; extensions arrive only by equivalence-registry amendment.

**GI-OBJ-8 (completion-dependent mention identity).** For any Step-8 affected_object whose canonical mention depends on completion, coreference, or antecedent resolution (completion_status = context_completed or equivalent referential-dependency metadata), object equivalence is determined from the referential completion evidence BEFORE surface-form equality may be used: same unique antecedent → OE-2 may confirm (never OE-1 as primary proof); different unique antecedents → object equivalence = false; ambiguous/unresolved antecedent on either side → object equivalence = unresolved (→ possibly_same_claim · object_coreference_ambiguous where applicable). Prohibited inference: same anaphoric/generic surface string → same object. Step-8 completion rules (I-44/I-45; OR-COREF-1) are consumed, never modified.

No external scientific knowledge participates. Strong evidence contributes to SAME_GAP only through the full GI-CORE-1 conjunction.

## 42.12 Insufficient object-equivalence evidence

**GI-OBJ-4 (closed insufficiency list).** NEVER sufficient alone: similar meaning; lexical similarity; same head noun; same `about`; same topic; semantic embeddings; LLM belief in synonymy; external ontology similarity; same external registry category; same citation; same theory family; same method family. Frozen anchor: "absorptive capacity" vs "knowledge assimilation capability" without explicit manuscript equivalence → equivalence unproven → at most `possibly_same_claim` · `object_equivalence_unproven`. Human verification or explicit manuscript evidence may later establish it; Step 9 never does so from similarity.

## 42.13 Explicit aliasing

**GI-ART-2 (explicit-alias rule).** Manuscript-asserted equivalence ("employee involvement, hereafter engagement"; "Model A (the adaptation model)"; "the resource-based view (RBV)") MAY license object equivalence iff ALL hold: (1) the alias relation itself is source-grounded (span-traceable); (2) both expressions are explicitly linked by the aliasing construction; (3) no contradictory local usage exists; (4) the aliasing does not force incompatible object_kind/artifact_kind. The alias evidence trace is recorded internally (spans of the aliasing construction); no external ontology is created or consulted.

## 42.14 External resolution boundary

**GI-OBJ-5.** `object_resolution_link` lives only in the mutable processing envelope (I-35/I-43) and can change after semantic freeze; therefore it MUST NOT be the sole basis of canonical gap identity, which stays grounded in manuscript assertion. External resolution may serve as CORROBORATING evidence only when a strong class (OE-1..OE-6, with OE-1 subject to the GI-OBJ-8 referential-dependency constraint) independently holds; external resolution never repairs ambiguous coreference — i.e., it can support a decision already licensed, never license one. A later registry relink can therefore never silently merge or split frozen gaps: identity decisions cite only span-grounded evidence.

## 42.15 Relationship-object equivalence

**GI-RELATIONSHIP-1 (structural comparison).** For relationship objects, compare the Step-8 internal metadata: argument_1, argument_2, direction, predicate, relational_qualifier, args_complete. Argument identity must itself be source-grounded (each argument pair satisfying an OE class). **GI-RELATIONSHIP-2 (direction).** directed vs undirected is a material difference: relationship(X,Y) directed ≠ relationship(X,Y) undirected — never automatically identical. Routing (frozen): when direction is fully explicit and the source-grounded core arguments match, the pair is DISTINCT_GAP and routes via POSSIBLE-B to `possibly_same_claim` · `relationship_direction_difference` (a broader underlying relationship gap stays plausible while canonical identity is blocked); it is NEVER simultaneously routed to `qualified_vs_unqualified_relationship` — direction routing and qualifier routing are separate. **GI-RELATIONSHIP-3 (qualifier — frozen two-case routing).** A materially different `relational_qualifier` blocks confirmed identity; the routing is deterministic and exclusive. CASE A — the qualifier difference is explicit and fully resolved (Step-8 metadata confirms same core arguments, same direction, an explicit qualifier on exactly one side): → DISTINCT_GAP → `related_claim` · `qualified_vs_unqualified_relationship`; NO `possibly_same_claim` is also emitted. "how X affects Y" vs "how X affects Y through M" and "X→Y" vs "X→Y under condition Z" are CASE A. CASE B — the qualifier identity/attachment/content is unresolved or the representation is incomplete: → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED → `possibly_same_claim` · `relationship_qualifier_difference`. One pair can never take both routes for the same structural fact. **GI-RELATIONSHIP-4 (partial structures).** args_complete = partial is never merged with a richer structure unless equivalence is independently source-grounded (e.g., verbatim proposition identity, P-EQ-1) — structural poverty never licenses collapse. **GI-RELATIONSHIP-5 (predicate normalization registry — closed).** Derivational predicate variants are equivalent ONLY via this frozen registry: EQ-PRED-1: effect_on ↔ affects (directed, a1→a2 preserved); EQ-PRED-2: impact_on ↔ affects (directed); EQ-PRED-3: association_between ↔ associated_with (undirected); EQ-PRED-4: relationship_between ↔ link_to (undirected). Nothing else. Open-ended synonym matching of relational predicates is prohibited; extensions arrive only by equivalence-registry amendment (§42.52).

## 42.16 About compatibility

**GI-OBJ-6.** For knowledge artifacts, `about` participates in equivalence evidence when present: (A) conflicting explicit `about` content blocks confirmed equivalence where material; the `about_conflict` possibly_same route requires an actual referent COLLISION (same unique designation + conflicting about, e.g. "Model M of trust" vs "Model M of commitment" → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED → `possibly_same_claim` · `about_conflict` + upstream/source-use review), whereas a source that clearly establishes two distinct artifacts despite parallel wording → DISTINCT_GAP with no possibly_same link — the trigger is collision ambiguity, never the about difference alone; (B) same `about` alone is INSUFFICIENT to prove the same artifact (two different models of trust exist); (C) `about` present on one record and absent on the other does not automatically block identity IF independent strong evidence (OE-3/OE-4) proves the same artifact; (D) absent `about` is never a wildcard subject matter. Minimal pairs: MP-13 (about X vs about Y → distinct), MP-14 (about-present vs about-absent + same proper designation → equivalence may confirm).

## 42.17 Complete proposition equivalence

**COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(record) — Step-9 INTERNAL comparison interface (not a canonical schema field; consumes already-frozen inputs only).** Definition: { `proposition.assertion_span_refs` (GS-1.3: required, full wording preserved, I-27) · `proposition.normalized.text` (required per I-2: the frozen trace-grounded completed/normalized proposition representation) · `proposition.normalized.source_trace_refs` (required, ≥1, ⊆ assertion refs ∪ permitted support refs — I-2) · `proposition.normalized.normalization_method` · the record's `context_support` where `explicitness = context_supported` (I-26) · the source spans grounding that completion (I-39) }. Semantics: (A) `assertion_span_refs` = the authoritative LOCAL asserting wording — an anchor, not necessarily the entire completed proposition; (B) `normalized.text` = the frozen completed representation; (C) `context_support` supplies only linguistic support already authorized upstream; (D) every semantic element of the completed content MUST be source-grounded by assertion spans and/or permitted support traces (the I-2 ⊆-constraint IS this invariant); (E) Step 9 NEVER creates proposition content; (F) Step 9 NEVER completes from world knowledge, manuscript objectives, RQs, contributions, later sections, or semantic guessing. Example of why the anchor is not the whole: support "Prior work has documented several coordination mechanisms." + assertion "These mechanisms remain unexplored in distributed settings." — the frozen completed proposition may identify "coordination mechanisms" via permitted context support; Step 9 compares that complete representation, not the fragment alone. **Anti-erasure rule (frozen):** completion may ADD source-grounded referential content required to interpret the assertion; it may NEVER remove material proposition content present in the assertion span — an attribution frame, modality, restriction, temporal phrase, conditional, negation, or qualifier inside `assertion_span_refs` cannot be silently deleted by normalization for identity purposes. **Boundaries:** LINGUISTIC COMPLETION ("These mechanisms" → unique antecedent, source-grounded) ≠ SEMANTIC PARAPHRASE ("family firms" vs "family-owned businesses" stays P-EQ-3 absent a registered entry — context_support is never a synonym engine); COMPLETION ≠ SYNTHESIS (GI-MERGE-2 holds: no generalizing, specializing, combining, restriction-union, latent scope, hidden constructs, or synthetic gaps — completion is lossless and source-grounded). **Step-8 boundary (frozen):** Step 8 owns affected_object, object mention, object coreference, object completion, relationship structure, and object traces; Step 8 does NOT own proposition equivalence, removal of material proposition wording, full proposition reconstruction, or deciding whether two propositions are the same. Step 9 consumes Step-8 object outputs alongside the frozen proposition representation.

**GI-PROP-1 (mandatory fourth conjunct).** After exact relation, exact knowledge_kind, and confirmed object equivalence, Step 9 establishes PROPOSITION_EQUIVALENT over COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(A) and COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(B). The comparison must preserve: local material wording in `assertion_span_refs`; the frozen normalized/completed proposition content; source-grounded context-supported completion; all material restrictions and qualifications. The comparison never reduces to relation + kind + affected_object, and never reduces to `assertion_span_refs` alone when upstream completion is required to interpret the proposition. Comparing only the triple is prohibited; the complete-proposition test is what keeps un-structured contextual wording identity-relevant without any scope schema.

## 42.18 Material proposition content

**GI-PROP-2.** Material proposition content = source-grounded wording that changes the conditions under which the deficiency is asserted to hold, including (without structural classification): population restriction; setting/context restriction; geography; temporal restriction; level/context qualification; conditional relationship qualifiers; contrastive restriction; subpopulation; artifact qualification; any other source-grounded limitation on the proposition. These remain proposition WORDING — no `population =`, `geography =`, or `time =` field is ever created.

## 42.19 Contextual wording differences

**GI-PROP-3 (contextual difference rule; frozen).** Same triple + confirmed same object + materially different restrictive contextual wording ≠ SAME_GAP. Frozen anchor (schema V10): "…limited among frontline workers." vs "…limited among senior managers." → distinct canonical gaps, reciprocal `possibly_same_claim` with `unmerged_basis_code = contextual_wording_difference`. Different geography ("in Europe" vs "in Asia") and different temporal restriction ("before 2020" vs "after 2020") behave identically.

## 42.20 Non-identity-bearing wrappers

**GI-PROP-4 (closed wrapper list — two tiers).** (A) Ordinary non-semantic wrappers, ignorable for proposition identity when source semantics are unchanged: punctuation; capitalization; frozen safe determiner normalization; parenthetical citation strings; citation ordering; section location; sentence-initial discourse connectives ("However," "Nevertheless," "Therefore,") that do not alter the asserted deficiency; explicit restatement markers such as "this gap" or "as noted above", treatable as non-identity-bearing ONLY when the frozen upstream proposition representation, including its permitted context-support traces, already resolves the proposition sufficiently for deterministic comparison (Step 8 resolves objects, never whole propositions — GI-OBJ-8 governs object completion separately). (B) Registered carrier-equivalence normalization — NOT a generic wrapper: deficiency-carrier wording is ignorable ONLY where the carrier pair is licensed by a registered EQ-DEF entry under GI-PROP-8 AND the remaining proposition_identity_residue is equivalent; equality of relation and knowledge_kind alone NEVER licenses carrier removal or proposition equivalence. Example: "little is known about X" vs "evidence about X is limited" is ignorable ONLY because EQ-DEF-3 + same LIMITED/evidence + confirmed same object + equivalent residue; the same construction pair without a registered entry routes to P-EQ-3 (GT-POSS-06). NEVER mechanically ignorable: negation; modality; quantified restrictions; conditionals; temporal restrictions; context restrictions; relationship qualifiers; attribution frames (attribution is not a wrapper — GI-CORE-6).

## 42.21 Proposition identity residue

**GI-RESIDUE-1.** `proposition_identity_residue` is a Step-9 INTERNAL comparison aid — not a canonical field, never stored as semantic content, never a scope schema. It is built from COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(record), not from `assertion_span_refs` alone. Construction: (1) preserve the complete source-grounded proposition representation; (2) recognize the already-frozen deficiency carrier/predicate; (3) recognize the already-extracted affected object; (4) apply only registered wrapper / EQ-DEF / P-EQ rules (§42.20, §42.22); (5) preserve every remaining material source-grounded restriction or qualification verbatim. Context-supported completion may resolve an anaphoric referent; it may not erase local material wording (anti-erasure rule). Residue remains Step-9 INTERNAL and is never added to the canonical schema. Example: "Little is known about X among nurses." and "Evidence about X is limited among nurses." both reduce (given matching triple/object) to residue "among nurses" → equivalent; "…among senior managers." yields a materially different residue → not equivalent.

## 42.22 Proposition equivalence tiers

**GI-PROP-5 (closed tiers).**
- **P-EQ-1 — deterministic equivalence** (eligible for SAME_GAP): exact proposition after safe normalization; different deficiency-carrier wording ONLY via a registered EQ-DEF entry (GI-PROP-8) with same triple/object and identical residue — same Step-6 relation alone NEVER establishes proposition equivalence; citation-only differences; restatement/coreference forms whose frozen normalized proposition representation already equals the compared proposition.
- **P-EQ-2 — licensed grammatical paraphrase** (SAME_GAP only via a registered lossless transformation; closed registry): T-ACT-PASS: active↔passive of the same predication with identical arguments; T-NOMV: nominal↔verbal alternation of the same predication with identical arguments ("translation of research into practice remains limited" ↔ "translating research into practice remains limited"); T-RESTR-POS: pre-/post-position of the same restriction ("Among nurses, evidence about X is limited." ↔ "Evidence about X is limited among nurses."). Nothing else; extensions only by equivalence-registry amendment.

## 42.23 SAME_GAP decision

**GI-MERGE-1.** Confirmed SAME_GAP → exactly ONE canonical gap_record survives. No `same_claim` link exists or is invented: the frozen schema represents confirmed identity solely as one canonical record + multiple source-evidence spans. The additional assertion(s) attach as `restatement` (or another already-licensed role, §42.40). No second canonical gap survives to represent the same identity.

**GI-MERGE-2 (no semantic synthesis).** Merging NEVER synthesizes a new proposition. Prohibited: writing a combining paraphrase; creating a more general or more specific object; unioning or intersecting qualifiers; inferring shared scope; inferring a latent common construct; inferring a common scientific proposition. The canonical semantic payload comes from ONE source-grounded representative instance (§42.38); other instances attach as restatements. MERGE ≠ SEMANTIC SYNTHESIS.

## 42.24 Distinct-gap decision

**GI-CORE-7.** DISTINCT_GAP arises from any definitive blocker: relation mismatch (GI-REL-1); kind mismatch (GI-KIND-1); confirmed object non-equivalence; confirmed material proposition difference. Distinctness is a positive determination, not a fallback for uncertainty — and it does NOT imply related-or-no-link exclusively: after distinctness, Step 9 performs Phase-II non-merged link evaluation (§42.42), which may yield `possibly_same_claim` under a registered POSSIBLE-B basis (e.g., contextual_wording_difference, proposition_qualification_difference, relationship_direction_difference), `related_claim` under a registered §42.45 basis, or no link.

## 42.25 Identity unresolved

**GI-UNCERTAIN-1.** IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED arises when identity is neither confirmed nor definitively blocked: object equivalence unproven (GI-OBJ-4); ambiguous completion (`object_coreference_ambiguous`); kind-collision routing (GI-OBJ-2/GI-ART-1); P-EQ-3 paraphrase; partial-vs-richer relationship without independent grounding; insufficient trace to compare (`insufficient_trace`). Consequence: both canonical records remain; reciprocal `possibly_same_claim` with the governing POSSIBLE-A basis code; the pair stays reviewable. Unresolved never merges and never auto-resolves by similarity. IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED is one route to `possibly_same_claim`, not the only one: definitively DISTINCT pairs may also receive the link via POSSIBLE-B (§42.27).

## 42.26 Restatement attachment

**GI-REST-1.** Step 5 admits restatement GAP candidates; Step 9 decides whether a restatement is SAME_GAP by applying GI-CORE-1 to the frozen upstream outputs: Step-6 relation; Step-6 knowledge_kind; Step-8 affected_object; COMPLETE_PROPOSITION_INPUT(record). If confirmed: no new canonical record; the assertion span attaches with `gap_evidence.role = restatement`. A phrase such as "This gap persists." never itself proves identity — its affected object and proposition must already be source-groundedly recoverable from frozen upstream completion/context-support traces. If the complete proposition remains unresolved: never merge; route conservatively under the applicable POSSIBLE-A basis (`insufficient_trace` or `object_coreference_ambiguous` per trigger).

## 42.27 Possibly-same claims

**GI-POSSIBLE-1 (two legal routes; every route requires a registered `unmerged_basis_code`).**
- **POSSIBLE-A — unresolved identity.** The strict identity decision itself is unresolved: unproven object synonymy; ambiguous coreference; insufficient trace; unlicensed semantic paraphrase; unresolved qualifier comparison. → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED → `possibly_same_claim`.
- **POSSIBLE-B — canonical distinctness with plausible shared/core gap.** Identity is definitively DISTINCT because of a material qualification, direction, context, or other registered distinction, yet a single broader/core-gap reading remains meaningfully plausible: same X among nurses vs managers; same X in Europe vs Asia; explicit direction mismatch over the same core arguments; materially different modality over the same proposition. → DISTINCT_GAP → `possibly_same_claim`.

`possibly_same_claim` therefore does NOT always mean "canonical identity is unresolved"; it may mean "definitively not the same canonical gap, but registered evidence keeps a common core gap plausible". It is never a merge — both canonical records persist — and no possibly_same link exists without a registered basis. **GI-POSSIBLE-2 (reciprocity).** possibly_same_claim is represented symmetrically: A→B requires B→A with the SAME basis code; duplicate reciprocal links are prohibited (one link per direction per pair per basis).

## 42.28 Unmerged basis codes

Owned closed vocabulary for `claim_link.unmerged_basis_code` — full registry in §42.44. Codes: contextual_wording_difference · object_equivalence_unproven · object_coreference_ambiguous · object_kind_conflict · artifact_kind_conflict · about_conflict · relationship_argument_difference · relationship_direction_difference · relationship_qualifier_difference · proposition_qualification_difference · semantic_paraphrase_unverified · insufficient_trace · cluster_consistency_conflict. No other value is legal; no code duplicates another's trigger.

## 42.29 Related claims

**GI-RELATED-1.** `link_type = related_claim` applies ONLY between confirmed-DISTINCT gaps with a meaningful source-grounded semantic relation licensed by a registered §42.30 basis. **GI-RELATED-2.** Topical similarity alone NEVER creates a related_claim; no link is the default between distinct gaps. **GI-RELATED-3 (single primary basis).** One `related_claim` pair carries exactly ONE primary `relation_basis_code`; when several registered bases apply, select by fixed precedence: 1. explicit_manuscript_relation · 2. artifact_subject_matter_relation · 3. proposition_relationship_relation · 4. qualified_vs_unqualified_relationship · 5. same_object_different_relation · 6. same_object_different_knowledge_kind · 7. practice_alignment_family. No duplicate semantically redundant links between the same pair. Eligible patterns (each per its basis): same object + different relation; same object + different knowledge_kind; qualified vs unqualified relationship; artifact and its explicitly attached subject-matter gap; proposition-artifact and relationship gaps over explicitly identical content; practice-alignment gaps over distinct parts of the same research→practice structure; an explicit manuscript statement relating two gaps.

## 42.30 Relation basis codes

Owned closed vocabulary for `claim_link.relation_basis_code` — full registry in §42.45. Retained codes (7): same_object_different_relation · same_object_different_knowledge_kind · qualified_vs_unqualified_relationship · artifact_subject_matter_relation · proposition_relationship_relation · practice_alignment_family · explicit_manuscript_relation. **Rejected candidate:** `contextual_variant` — not registered because materially different contextual restrictions already have a complete deterministic route: strict canonical identity = DISTINCT_GAP, plus `possibly_same_claim` with `unmerged_basis_code = contextual_wording_difference` (POSSIBLE-B). The possibly_same link means a broader/shared core gap remains plausible despite CONFIRMED canonical distinctness; it does NOT mean strict identity is unresolved. No separate related-claim basis is needed, and contextual variants are never converted to related_claim.

## 42.31 Link symmetry

**GI-LINK-1 (frozen).** possibly_same_claim → symmetric (reciprocal pair, same basis, no duplicates). related_claim → symmetric; no directional related type is invented here. reason_of → directional: REASON GAP → PARENT GAP only (schema V9; `source_persistence_reason_id` on the reason-side link). Symmetric A→B without B→A is an invalid state; generators must emit both or neither. **GI-LINK-2 (exclusivity).** For the SAME pair and the SAME underlying structural/comparison basis, `possibly_same_claim` and `related_claim` are mutually exclusive. Routing priority: 1. SAME_GAP → merge, no link; 2. unresolved identity or registered plausible-core variant → possibly_same; 3. confirmed distinct + deterministic registered relation → related; 4. otherwise no link. `reason_of` is semantically independent (persistence causation, not identity similarity) and MAY coexist with one symmetric possibly_same/related link on the same pair.

## 42.32 Persistence reasons

**GI-REASON-1.** A persistence reason creates another gap only when it independently asserts another prior-knowledge deficiency — that determination arrives from upstream (I-16: `spawned_claim_ref` ABSENT = no independent deficiency; Step 9 never decides deficiency-hood). Step 9 decides only the spawned instance's identity: (A) new canonical gap; (B) identical to an existing canonical gap; (C) identical to the parent; (D) possibly same / related to existing gaps.

## 42.33 Spawned-gap reconciliation

**GI-REASON-2.** A genuinely independent reason-deficiency yields a canonical gap carrying `claim_link{link_type: reason_of, target_gap_id: parent, source_persistence_reason_id: PR-n}` — direction REASON → PARENT, never reversed (V9 architecture). If the spawned assertion is SAME_GAP with an ALREADY EXISTING canonical Gap B elsewhere in the manuscript: no duplicate canonical B; the spawned instance merges into existing B under full GI-CORE-1; canonical B then carries the `reason_of` link to the parent; the persistence-reason provenance (`persistence_reasons` entry, `spawned_claim_ref`, `source_persistence_reason_id`) is preserved through the merge. Two distinct persistence reasons spawning the same Gap B yield one canonical B with two `reason_of` links (distinct `source_persistence_reason_id` values) — legal and required.

## 42.34 Self-reason edge case

**GI-REASON-3.** If the spawned reason assertion is SAME_GAP with its own PARENT: no second canonical record is created, and NO `reason_of` self-link is created — I-31 makes `target_gap_id = own gap_id` structurally invalid, and a self-reason relation is semantically empty. Deterministic handling: attach the reason's assertion span to the parent as `restatement` (or `corroborating` where upstream already so classified it); preserve the `persistence_reasons` entry and its source trace unchanged; reconcile `spawned_claim_ref` before semantic freeze per the State-Machine/Schema constraints (the pre-promotion candidate is not promoted to a duplicate record; the reference resolves to the parent's canonical identity). Provenance is never silently deleted.

## 42.35 Identity clustering

**GI-CLUSTER-1 (full-compatibility invariant).** A canonical same-gap cluster may contain a member ONLY when that member is identity-compatible with EVERY other member under the full SAME_GAP rule (pairwise complete linkage). Naive union-find over arbitrary positive pairwise edges is prohibited.

## 42.36 No transitive bridge merging

**GI-CLUSTER-2.** Only CONFIRMED SAME_GAP edges cluster, and confirmation is available only through deterministic tiers (P-EQ-1, registered P-EQ-2) over deterministic object equivalence — relations that are equivalence-grade by construction (normal-form comparisons), so confirmed edges cannot form a pass-pass-block triangle when rules are correctly applied. IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED edges (possibly_same) NEVER contribute to clustering. If a confirmed-edge triangle with a blocking third pair nonetheless appears (rule misapplication or trace corruption): no member of the triangle merges; all three pairs route to review with reciprocal `possibly_same_claim` · `cluster_consistency_conflict`; nothing is force-resolved. Frozen anchor: A = "X among nurses", B = the same proposition with the restriction pre-posed (registered T-RESTR-POS, P-EQ-2), C = "X among managers" — A+B cluster; C stays out; no B-mediated bridge admits C because C conflicts with A under GI-PROP-3.

## 42.37 Fixpoint / Step-4 coordination

**GI-CLUSTER-3 (order invariance).** Same manuscript + same extracted instances + same rule versions ⇒ the same canonical partition regardless of detector order, candidate arrival order, thread/concurrency order, or batch order. Guaranteed by construction: the partition is the set of equivalence classes of the deterministic confirmed-SAME_GAP relation over the closed universe, and representative selection (§42.38) uses no arrival-order input. **GI-CLUSTER-4.** Final identity routing is stable only when: the run's candidate universe is closed per Step 4 (`CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE_CLOSED`, current epoch); all identity comparisons are complete; no pending merge/attachment changes remain; the partition reaches a deterministic fixpoint. `identity_routing_freeze_stable` cites the current closure epoch (SM v1.3); Step 9 owns identity rules only, never lifecycle orchestration, and redefines no Step-4 state or event.

## 42.38 Canonical representative selection

**GI-REP-1 (frozen precedence; arrival-order-free).** Among confirmed same-gap instances, select ONE representative by, in order: (1) `explicitness = explicit` over `context_supported`; (2) locally grounded affected object over completion-dependent grounding (Step-8 completion_status local over context_completed), where metadata is available; (3) complete relationship representation over partial (args_complete), where applicable; (4) otherwise, earliest primary-assertion source position in the manuscript (lowest primary span char_start — deterministic, non-semantic, document-ordered); (5) final tie-break: lexicographically lowest primary `span_id` (stable lexical ID). Assessment: steps 1–3 prefer the least-inference-dependent payload; step 4 is retained because it is deterministic and manuscript-grounded; nothing simpler would break ties among verbatim repetitions. PROHIBITED inputs: LLM confidence; scientific quality; citation count; journal prestige; section prestige; semantic "importance"; arrival order.

## 42.39 Canonical payload after merge

**GI-REP-2 (selection ≠ identity).** A pair must first pass SAME_GAP; only then does representative selection run. Precedence never decides identity: an explicit assertion does not absorb a context-supported assertion unless identity independently passes. **GI-REP-3.** The representative supplies the ENTIRE canonical semantic payload — relation, knowledge_kind, affected_object, explicitness/context support, proposition representation — unless a frozen schema rule explicitly permits otherwise. No hybrid payload is synthesized. Other instances contribute restatement spans, source provenance, permitted evidence roles, and lineage; no source evidence disappears (**GI-TRACE-1**).

## 42.40 Evidence roles after merge

**GI-REST-2.** After merge exactly one canonical `primary` gap-evidence assertion remains — the representative's. Deterministic handling: two merged primaries → representative's span stays primary, the other becomes `restatement`; explicit + context-supported → per GI-REP-1 the explicit instance is representative, the other's assertion becomes restatement; repeated introduction/discussion assertion → later-position instance's span becomes restatement (identity established by §42.17–42.22, never by section); identical wording twice → same, tie broken by GI-REP-1(4–5); anaphoric restatement → its completed assertion span attaches as restatement. Spans already classified upstream as `corroborating` remain corroborating; Step 9 never converts non-gap evidence into a GAP to enlarge the span set, and never redefines the frozen role system.

## 42.41 Section/citation invariance

**GI-PROP-6 (citation invariance).** Citation differences alone neither create nor block identity: "Little is known about X (Smith, 2022)." vs "…(Jones, 2024)." merge when everything non-citation is equivalent (wrappers, §42.20). Whether either citation supports the gap is downstream; citation lists and citation-resolution IDs are never identity keys. **GI-PROP-7 (section invariance).** The same gap asserted in Abstract, Introduction, Literature Review, Discussion, or Conclusion is one gap when full identity passes; section location alone never creates a new gap and never blocks a merge; material proposition differences still block regardless of section.

## 42.42 Identity decision procedure

**GI-PREC-1 (deterministic two-phase procedure).**
PHASE I — STRICT CANONICAL IDENTITY. GI-1 Confirm comparison universe (§42.5). GI-2 Compare exact relation — mismatch → strict identity = DISTINCT_GAP; skip the remaining SAME_GAP conjunct evaluation (evidence later required by link routing is gathered in Phase II, never suppressed). GI-3 Compare exact knowledge_kind — mismatch → DISTINCT_GAP. GI-4 Evaluate object equivalence — false → DISTINCT_GAP; unresolved → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED; confirmed → continue. GI-5 Evaluate complete-proposition equivalence — confirmed → SAME_GAP; materially false → DISTINCT_GAP; unresolved → IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED.
PHASE II — NON-MERGED LINK ROUTING. GI-6 For every non-SAME pair, evaluate registered possibly_same bases (POSSIBLE-A for unresolved identity; POSSIBLE-B for distinct-with-plausible-core), evaluating ONLY the additional evidence those bases require. GI-7 Evaluate registered related bases — each basis names its own evidence (e.g., same_object_different_relation and same_object_different_knowledge_kind REQUIRE confirmed link-side object equivalence; qualified_vs_unqualified_relationship REQUIRES confirmed matched core relationship + explicit resolved qualifier); bases are never inferred from descriptive similarity. GI-8 Enforce GI-LINK-2 exclusivity and GI-RELATED-3 precedence: possibly_same governs while strict equivalence remains unresolved or a POSSIBLE-B basis applies; related governs when distinctness is established and the inter-gap relation is deterministic; one structural fact never yields both.
GI-9 SAME_GAP → assign canonical cluster (GI-CLUSTER-1). GI-10 Validate cluster consistency (GI-CLUSTER-2). GI-11 Select representative (GI-REP-1). GI-12 Attach restatement/provenance spans (GI-REST-1/2; GI-TRACE-1). GI-13 Emit persistence-reason links (GI-REASON-1/2/3; GI-LINK-1). GI-14 Re-run until the partition reaches the Step-4-compatible fixpoint (GI-CLUSTER-4). No runtime candidate detection is introduced.
