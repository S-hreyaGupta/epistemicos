<objective>
Prove the third condition: the gate fails and names the fixture path when a fixture
is unreadable — and prove it without ever touching the working tree, by breaking the
fixture inside a throwaway copy of `HEAD`.

Purpose: PROOF-03 is the condition that cannot be reproduced by an environment
variable. It needs a filesystem change, and D-07 records the measurement that removed
the obvious way to make one: `segment` and `gate` run concurrently, so mutating the
real `demo.md` corrupts a file another package is reading and the failure lands
somewhere unrelated to PROOF-03. This plan applies the tree materialiser 03-01 built
and adds the one condition-specific defeat, plus the differential control against a
copy defeated twice.

Output: `TestPROOF03_GateNamesUnreadableFixture` with both subtests and its
`breakFixture` helper. After this plan all three conditions are proven and all three
SC-5 controls exist.

## Cost, from CONTEXT.md's measurements — not re-estimated

PROOF-03's shape was measured at **18.2s warm** and **21s cold after a warm parent**
(D-05's tables) — the most expensive of the three, because unlike PROOF-01 and
PROOF-02 the nested gate gets past `env-preflight` and `migrate` and fails inside
`make test`. Its control adds one doubly-defeated run of the same shape. Adding the
fixture-*absence* case as a second subtest was rejected at roughly 18 to 21 seconds
per extra nested gate, for something GATE-03 does not ask for (D-09).

## The one genuine coupling left, recorded rather than treated as closed

D-17 names it explicitly, and it belongs at the top of this plan rather than buried in
an assertion comment. PROOF-03 binds to the path suffix
`core/domain/segment/testdata/demo.md`. That is a coupling to *where the fixture
lives* — which is exactly what GATE-03 names, so it is the right coupling — but it is
still a coupling, and it is the only one this phase did not eliminate. The suffix
rather than either full spelling is what makes it correct in both directions:

- Move the fixture, and both the on-disk path and the call-site literal change, so the
  suffix stops matching and the assertion fails. **Correct** — the requirement's
  subject moved.
- Change only the call site's relative depth (`../../../` becomes something else) and
  the suffix survives. **Also correct** — the file did not move.

It is recorded here so that a future reader who finds this assertion failing knows
which of the two situations they are in before deciding whether the proof or the
requirement is wrong.
</objective>
